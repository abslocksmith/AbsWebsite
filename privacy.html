Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information ABS Locksmiths collects when you contact us or use this website, how we use it, and the rights you have over it. Last updated 27 September 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by ABS Locksmiths Ltd (trading as "ABS Locksmiths"), a mobile locksmith based in Ilford and serving East London. We are the "data controller" responsible for your personal information. You can reach us by email at info@abslocksmith.co.uk or by phone on 07990 827063.

What information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us. When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, phone number, and — if you provide them — your email address, the service you're interested in, and any details in your message. If you call or email us directly, we hold whatever you tell us so we can respond and carry out the work.

We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies on this website, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

How we use your information

We use the details you provide only to respond to your enquiry, arrange and carry out locksmith work, and keep a record of the job. We rely on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about our services, and, once you ask us to carry out work, on taking steps to enter into and perform a contract with you. We will not use your details to send you marketing unless you have specifically asked us to.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information or share it for marketing. We use a small number of trusted service providers to run the website:

  • Web3Forms processes our contact form and forwards your enquiry to our email inbox so we can reply.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used on the site. When a page loads, your device requests these fonts from Google's servers, which involves sharing your IP address with Google.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and processes standard server request information.

We may also disclose information if the law requires us to.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies of its own and does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Because of this, there is no cookie consent banner — there are no non-essential cookies to consent to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and job records only for as long as we need them to provide our service, answer follow-up questions, and meet our legal and tax obligations, after which we delete them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to or restrict how we use it. To make a request, email us at info@abslocksmith.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will appear on this page with a revised date.
